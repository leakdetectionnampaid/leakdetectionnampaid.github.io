Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs of a hidden water leak?

The common ones are a water bill that rises without any change in use, a water meter that keeps moving while every fixture is off, damp or warm patches on floors, musty odors, peeling paint, and the sound of running water in a quiet house. Any one of these is worth a diagnostic visit.

Can you really find a leak without tearing into walls or floors?

In most cases, yes. FLIR infrared thermography reveals the temperature patterns that escaping water leaves behind, acoustic detectors pick up the sound of a pressurized leak through slab and soil, and moisture meters confirm exactly where the water has traveled. If anything does need opening, it is limited to the one spot that matters.

Are you available at night and on weekends?

Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. If water is actively spreading, call (208) 218-9616 and we will get a specialist moving right away.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Nampa and serve homes and businesses throughout the city and nearby Canyon County communities. If you are just outside that area, call and ask — we will tell you straight away whether we can reach you.

What should I do while I wait, if water is actively leaking?

If it is safe to do so, shut off your main water valve to limit further damage, keep clear of any electrical hazards near the water, and move valuables out of the affected area. Then call us — pinpointing the source is much easier before everything dries out.
